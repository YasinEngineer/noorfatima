Admission Process

1

Submit Inquiry Form

Fill out the inquiry form on this page or visit our campus. Our team will contact you to discuss your academic needs and recommend the right program.

2

Free Trial Class

We encourage students to take a 2-day free trial class to experience our teaching methodology and campus environment before committing.

3

Registration & Assessment

Submit required documents (previous result cards, ID copy) and complete the registration form. A brief assessment may be conducted for O-Level/A-Level students.

4

Free Enrollment Confirmation

Secure your seat completely free of cost. You will be provided with a student ID and your batch schedule to start learning immediately.

Required Documents

  • 2 Passport size photographs
  • Copy of Student's B-Form / CNIC
  • Copy of Parent's / Guardian's CNIC
  • Copy of latest school result card
